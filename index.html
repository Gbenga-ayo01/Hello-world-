What I do

Websites designed around your business, your products and the people you want to reach.

  • Website design

    Clear layouts, considered typography and visual systems that give your business a professional online presence.

  • Frontend development

    Responsive websites built with HTML, CSS and JavaScript, with attention to usability, structure and browser performance.

  • E-commerce websites

    Online storefronts with product displays, shopping cart functionality and checkout experiences.

  • Website functionality

    Interactive features and backend integrations using JavaScript and Supabase, based on the requirements of your project.

  • Website improvements

    Refining existing websites through layout improvements, interface updates and additional functionality.

A straightforward process

A good website starts with understanding what the business actually needs.

  1. Discovery

    We discuss your business, your audience, your goals and the features your website needs.

  2. Design

    I establish the page structure, visual direction and user experience before moving into development.

  3. Development

    The approved design is translated into a responsive, functional website using the appropriate technologies.

  4. Review and delivery

    We review the completed website, address agreed revisions and prepare it for handover or launch.

Clear communication at every stage. No unnecessary complexity.
